SupportMail is designed to be a **powerful**, **modern**, **localized** and (mostly) **free modmail system** for Discord servers.

Read about what SupportMail has to offer [here](/features).

<LinkButton href="https://discord.gg/gv8jupwGtA">Support Server</LinkButton>

---

SupportMail is run by [LukeZ](https://thelukez.com) as a solo developer - this includes the bot itself, the homepage, the dashboard, the statuspage and this documentation.

This has a couple of implications:

- I'm no marketing team, so I don't have the time or resources to make SupportMail look like a flashy product and write marketing stuff. I focus on making SupportMail work well and be useful, not on making it look like a product that needs to be sold to you.
- I don't have the time and resources to make SupportMail a full-time job (as of now), so I have to make sure that SupportMail is sustainable. This means that some features will be behind a paid subscription, but the free plan will still give you everything you need to run support in your Discord server. The paid subscription is for people who want to support the project and get access to heavier features.
- Bugs and issues are fixed as quickly as possible, usually within a matter of hours or days, but sometimes it takes longer.
- Not because I'm a solo dev, but because how I am as a person, I want to be as transparent as possible about how SupportMail is run and what you can expect from it. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to me on the [support server](https://discord.gg/gv8jupwGtA) (use the correct channels please...)
- Features are usually shipped as soon as they are ready, but sometimes it takes a bit longer to make sure they are stable and work well.
- Because of the lack of resources, testing and running the bot is harder than you might think.
- Even though I like adding customization options where possible, I try to avoid adding stuff which just complicates things for me and doesn't add much value for the end user. This is why some features are not customizable (default messages for example).

Since I can only treat this as a side project, I can't guarantee that SupportMail will always be worked on (even though I do my best to make sure it is), but I can guarantee that I will always try to make SupportMail better and more useful for the community.
