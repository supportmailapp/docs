SupportMail talks to a lot of people who would rather read their own language than English. Every string you translate makes the bot a little friendlier for them, and you do not need to be a developer to help.

## How it works

We manage translations in **Thoth**, a small web app built for exactly this.

1. **Sign in** at [thoth.supportmail.dev](https://thoth.supportmail.dev) with your Discord account. There is no application form: everyone starts as a contributor.
2. **Pick a language.** German and French are open for translation right now.
3. **Suggest a fix or fill a gap.** Improve a string that reads oddly, or translate one that is still missing.
4. **A reviewer approves it.** Someone who knows the language checks your suggestion.
5. **It ships.** Approved translations make their way into the bot automatically. No git, no pull requests, no setup.

## Roles

- **Contributor:** suggests new or improved translations and follows their own submissions. This is where everyone starts.
- **Reviewer:** approves or rejects suggestions from everyone and can edit more complex strings.
- **Admin:** everything a reviewer can do, plus running the pipeline that ships translations to the bot.

## Credit

Contributors get credited in the [support server](https://help.supportmail.dev/). Your work is seen.

## Your language is not listed?

More languages are planned, and Spanish is likely next. If you would like to translate SupportMail into another language, ask in the [support server](https://help.supportmail.dev/) and let us know.
