Every slash command SupportMail has, grouped by area. **Who** is the default permission a member needs. Options in **bold** are required.

<Aside type="tip">
  Server admins can change who may use a command in **Server Settings →
  Integrations → SupportMail**, or on the <DashLink route="?next=/command-config">Command Config</DashLink> page of the dashboard.
</Aside>

| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <SlashCommand name="ticket-create" />           | Everyone         | **`server`**, `anonymous`                                       | [Contacting Support](/getting-support/contacting-support)     |
| <SlashCommand name="send" />                    | Timeout Members  | `user`, `message`, `anonym-override`                            | [Handling Tickets](/managing-support/handling-tickets)        |

### Debug

<SlashCommand name="debug" /> checks your setup and repairs broken configuration. Pick one `option`:

#### Check role integrity

Removes roles that no longer exist from your ticket pings, report moderators and immune lists.

#### Check user integrity

Removes users who are no longer in your server from those same lists.

#### Check channel setup

Checks that the ticket forum and the report alert channel still exist and that the bot can see them. A channel that was deleted is removed from your settings. A channel the bot can't see is kept, and you're told to grant **View Channel**.

#### Check permissions

Lists the permissions the bot is missing in those channels.

See [Common Issues](/troubleshooting/common-issues#hidden-channels-and-missing-permissions) for hidden channels.
