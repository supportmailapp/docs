If your server uses SupportMail, anyone with dashboard access (server owner, admins, managers) can submit a short testimonial explaining why. Approved testimonials may be featured on the SupportMail homepage. A server can only have **one** testimonial at a time.

## Submitting a testimonial

Go to <DashLink route="?next=/testimonial">Testimonial</DashLink> in the dashboard and fill in:

- **Your testimonial** - the main text, at least 20 characters.
- **Source** (optional) - who you are, e.g. "Owner of X" or "Community Manager".

By submitting, you agree to let SupportMail use your testimonial on its homepage and marketing materials.

## Editing or revoking

Once a testimonial exists for a server, only the person who submitted it can edit or revoke it - this keeps a single, consistent submission per server instead of staff overwriting each other. Everyone else with dashboard access sees a read-only view naming the submitter.

While pending approval, the submitter can edit its content and source, or revoke it entirely from the same page.

Once a testimonial is **approved**, it's live on the homepage and can no longer be edited or revoked from the dashboard. To make changes at that point, open a ticket on the [Support Server](https://help.supportmail.dev/) or email `contact@supportmail.dev`.

<Aside type="note">
  Anyone with dashboard access can submit a testimonial, but only its submitter
  can edit or revoke it afterward.
</Aside>
