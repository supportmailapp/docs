Feedback lets users rate their support experience when a ticket closes. It gives your team a simple signal of how support is going and doubles as a friendly closure notification for the user. This page is for staff - for the setup options, see [Ticket Feedback Configuration](/configuration/ticket-feedback-configuration).

## How it works

1. A ticket is [closed](/managing-support/handling-tickets#closing-tickets) while feedback is enabled.
2. The user gets a rating prompt in their DMs (plus any custom questions you added).
3. When they submit, they see your **thank-you message**, and their response is posted to your **feedback channel**.

Because the prompt arrives as the ticket closes, users always know their ticket was resolved - even if you closed it without a comment.

## What the user sees

The close message in the user's DMs gets five star buttons labelled **1** to **5**. The user picks a rating:

- If you have **no custom questions**, the rating is submitted immediately.
- If you have **custom questions**, tapping a star opens a short form for them to fill in.

After submitting, the buttons are disabled and the star they chose turns green, so it's clear their feedback was recorded. Users can also simply ignore the prompt - feedback is never forced.

## How feedback is presented to staff

Each submission is posted to your feedback channel as a compact card:

- **A colour-coded accent** reflects the rating at a glance - red for 1, orange for 2, yellow for 3, aqua for 4, and green for 5.
- **A heading links back to the ticket** and shows who left the feedback (their mention, or the anonymous alias for [anonymous tickets](/managing-support/handling-tickets#anonymous-tickets)) alongside the star rating.
- **The user's avatar** appears as a thumbnail, unless the ticket was anonymous.
- **Answers to custom questions** are rendered into a clean image attached to the card, so longer written feedback stays readable.

## Enabling feedback

Turn it on and choose the feedback channel, thank-you message and any custom questions under <DashLink route="?next=/feedback">Feedback</DashLink> in the dashboard. Full details are in [Ticket Feedback Configuration](/configuration/ticket-feedback-configuration).

<Aside type="note">
  Keep it light - a rating plus at most one or two questions get far more
  responses than a long survey.
</Aside>
