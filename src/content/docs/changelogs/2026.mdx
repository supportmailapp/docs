## 18.11.2026

### Changed: Opening tickets and writing to support

Contacting support by DM is now smoother. See [Contacting Support](/getting-support/contacting-support).

- Opening a ticket happens on one card that updates in place: pick the server and category, then press **Send**. If the category has a form, it appears when you press **Send**.
- With one open ticket, just write: your message goes straight to it, and a small "Sent to **Server**" line shows where it went.
- With two or more open tickets, the bot asks **Which ticket is this for?** once. Messages you send meanwhile are sent along with it.
- Sent a message to the wrong server? Press **Not for Server?** within 10 minutes to withdraw it and turn it into a new ticket.
- Panels and <SlashCommand name="ticket-create" /> now choose the server (and category) for you. Your next DM only needs **Send**.
- When you pick a server (or use a panel or command), the bot checks whether you can open a ticket there and tells you what is in the way.
- "Message Sent" is now **Ticket opened**, and shows the server, category and ticket ID.
- The close message is now **Ticket closed** and ends with "Writing again opens a new ticket."
- <SlashCommand name="reopen" /> now always tells the user that their ticket was reopened.
- Accepting or denying a close request no longer requires logging in.
- Several messages sent in quick succession are now all processed, in order.

### Removed: Automatic Redirect preference

The **Automatic Redirect** option in [Preferences](/getting-support/preferences) is gone, along with the "Create a new Ticket?" and "Continue in Ticket?" prompts. Messages now go to your only open ticket automatically, and with several open tickets you pick one per message.

### Fixed: Staff replies after a close request

Staff replies could be lost after a user cancelled a close request by writing again. They now arrive as expected.

## 14.11.2026

### New: Whitelabel (beta)

Servers in the beta can run SupportMail under their **own bot**: own name, avatar, banner, About me and presence, managed on the new **Whitelabel** page of the dashboard. Start, stop, restart, and switch back to the main bot at any time.

- Your bot serves only your server. Users DM it directly, with no login and no server picker.
- Panels are sent by whichever bot serves your server.

Access is limited during the beta. To request it, open a ticket in the [support server](https://help.supportmail.dev/). Whitelabel will become part of a future paid plan. See [Whitelabel](/configuration/whitelabel).

## 13.11.2026

### Fixed: Feedback settings

- Fixed a database issue that could stop a server's feedback settings from saving. Each server now has exactly one feedback configuration.

## 31.10.2026

### Changed: Hidden channels and permission warnings

Discord is starting to hide channels a bot isn't allowed to view. SupportMail now handles this:

- The dashboard shows such channels as **Unknown or hidden channel** instead of a raw ID.
- Saving Report Settings warns you if the bot is missing permissions in the alert channel.
- Sending a panel tells you if the bot can't view the channel or is missing permissions.
- <SlashCommand name="debug" /> keeps a hidden channel's configuration and tells you to grant **View Channel**, instead of deleting it.

See [Common Issues](/troubleshooting/common-issues#hidden-channels-and-missing-permissions).

## 29.10.2026

### New: Edit an existing panel

The Panel page has a new **Edit** tab: paste the link of a panel message and SupportMail updates it in place. If the message can't be edited (for example it was deleted), a new one is sent instead. See [Panel](/configuration/panel).

## 23.10.2026

### New: Setup wizard

Setting up tickets and reports is now a guided, step-by-step flow - in the dashboard and right inside Discord.

- **Dashboard:** new servers are taken straight into the wizard. If you skipped it, use **Continue setup** in the sidebar.
- **Discord:** `/setup tickets wizard` and `/setup reports wizard` walk you through every setting in a message only you can see. Re-running a wizard starts from your current settings.
- When the dashboard wizard creates a new ticket forum, you choose which roles and users can see it.
- Nothing is saved until you confirm on the final review screen. If a step fails while applying, you can retry without creating a second forum or panel.

See [Setup Wizard](/configuration/setup-wizard) for details.

### Changed: `/setup` commands

- `/setup tickets` is now `/setup tickets quick` (the one-shot setup with default settings).
- `/setup reports` used to only point you to the dashboard. It is now `/setup reports wizard`, a full setup inside Discord.

## 29.08.2026

### Authentication overhaul

- Rebuilt how dashboard logins work under the hood - sessions are now independent of your Discord connection, fixing several edge-case bugs around expired sessions and multi-device logins.
- Logging in on a new device or browser no longer signs you out elsewhere. Each session is now separate.
- Added a **Disconnect Discord** option (next to Log out) for fully revoking dashboard access to your Discord account, separate from just logging out of the dashboard.
- Role and permission changes (e.g., being promoted to mod/admin) now take effect immediately instead of requiring a re-login.
- This update requires everyone to log in again once. Your Discord bot setup and tickets are unaffected - only the dashboard session resets.

<details>
  <summary>For the curious - what actually changed</summary>

Previously, one database record did double duty as both your Discord connection (used by the bot) and your dashboard login session - and its "expiry" meant different things depending on which system last touched it. That's why logging in via the bot's `/login` command could quietly overwrite your dashboard session, and why logging in on a second device would silently invalidate the first.

I split those into two independent things:

- **Session** - just your dashboard login. Rotates freely, supports multiple devices, expires on a fixed schedule.
- **Grant** - your actual Discord connection, shared with the bot. Untouched by dashboard logouts, refreshed automatically instead of just expiring.

I also consolidated two separate, competing login implementations into one, and fixed a few quieter reliability bugs along the way (a cold-cache request that intermittently returned server errors, a login-replay edge case, and a banned-server redirect that was broken).

None of this changes what you can do on the dashboard - it changes how reliably it works.

</details>

## 15.08.2026

### New: Report attachments

<SlashCommand name="report user" /> and <SlashCommand name="report message" /> take an optional `attachment`, and reports from the right-click menu carry the reported message's attachments. Images and videos show up in a media gallery right in the report. See [Managing Reports](/managing-support/managing-reports).

## 07.08.2026

### Changed: `/ping`

<SlashCommand name="ping" /> now shows the server and cluster count across the whole bot, and links to the [status page](https://status.supportmail.dev/).

## 28.07.2026

### New: `/view` command

Moderators can now browse currently open tickets and unresolved reports right in Discord with `/view tickets` and `/view reports` - paginated, no dashboard trip needed.

See [Viewing Tickets and Reports](/managing-support/viewing-tickets-and-reports) for details.

### Changed: Granular DM notifications for reports

Report DMs are now three separate toggles instead of one all-or-nothing setting: DM on report creation, DM on report resolution, and DM to the reported user.
Each has its own **Sent for** (which outcomes trigger it) and **Included info** (what details it contains) controls.

See [Reports Configuration](/configuration/reports-configuration#dms-to-users) for details.

### Removed: Deprecated dashboard-redirect commands

A handful of slash subcommands that only pointed you to the dashboard have been removed - use the dashboard directly instead:

- `/config tickets` and `/config reports` (use [Ticket System Config](/configuration/ticket-system-config) / [Managing Reports](/managing-support/managing-reports) in the dashboard)
- `/config blacklist` (use the **Blacklist** page in the dashboard)
- `/config tags list` (use the **Tags** page in the dashboard, or [Tags](/configuration/tags))
- `/blacklist view` (use the **Blacklist** page in the dashboard)

## 09.07.2026

### New: Pausing Tickets

Staff can now pause bot activity in a single ticket with `/ticket pause`, and resume it with `/ticket unpause`. While paused, the bot ignores all messages in the ticket post - nothing is forwarded to the user.  
Messages _from_ the user still reach the channel.

<Aside type="tip">
  You can also use the prefix commands `-pause` and `-unpause` in the ticket
  channel to pause and unpause the ticket, respectively.
</Aside>

- New **Ask to Delete History on Unpause** setting under [Message Handling](/configuration/ticket-system-config#message-handling) - when enabled, unpausing prompts staff to bulk-delete the messages sent while the ticket was paused.

See [Pausing Tickets](/managing-support/handling-tickets#pausing-tickets) for details. **Off by default.**

### New: Delete Reported Message on Punish

When punishing a reported user (timeout, kick or ban) via a message report, the action modal now includes a checkbox to also delete the reported message.

## 07.07.2026

### New: Testimonials

Anyone with dashboard access can now submit a testimonial for their server directly from the dashboard. Approved testimonials may be featured on the SupportMail homepage.

- Submit a testimonial from the new **Testimonial** page in the dashboard - a server can only have one.
- Only the person who submitted it can edit or revoke it; everyone else sees a read-only view naming the submitter.
- Once approved, changes require contacting support.

See [Testimonials](/managing-support/testimonials) for details.

## 28.06.2026

### New: Support Schedule

You can now define **support hours** so tickets can only be opened when your team is available. Set a timezone offset and up to 3 time slots per day; outside those hours the bot tells users when to try again instead of opening a ticket.

See [Support Schedule](/configuration/support-schedule) for details. Configure it on the new **Schedule** page in the dashboard.

### Changed: Panel moved to the dashboard

Creating and editing your [Panel](/configuration/panel) now lives fully in the dashboard on the **Panel** page - build it, save it and send it to a channel from there. The old `/config panel` command has been removed.
