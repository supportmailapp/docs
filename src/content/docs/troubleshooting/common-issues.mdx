Quick fixes for the problems people run into most. If none of these help, [contact us](#still-stuck) and we'll take a look.

## For users

**The bot won't let me open a ticket / my DM goes unanswered**

- Make sure your DMs are open. In the server's **Privacy Settings**, enable _Direct Messages_ so the bot can reach you.
- You may need to authorize SupportMail with your Discord account first — the bot uses this to identify you from DMs.
- The server may have the ticket system **paused or disabled**, or you may be [blacklisted](/managing-support/blacklisting) from tickets. Ask the server's staff.

**The panel button does nothing**

- Check your DMs — the bot messages you there to continue. If nothing arrives, your DMs are likely closed.

## For moderators

**Messages I send in a ticket aren't reaching the user**

- **Automatic Forwarding** may be off. Either turn it on, or reply with `/send`, the `-r` flag, or by mentioning the bot. See [Handling Tickets](/managing-support/handling-tickets).
- If the user has since disabled their DMs or blocked the bot, delivery fails and you'll see an error.

**Report actions (timeout / kick / ban) fail**

- The bot needs the matching Discord permission **and** a role positioned above the target user. Check the role order and the bot's permissions.

## For admins

**Tickets or reports aren't working at all**

- Confirm the system is **enabled** and not **paused** in <DashLink route="?next=/ticket-settings">Ticket Settings</DashLink> / <DashLink route="?next=/report-settings">Report Settings</DashLink>.
- Reports require an **alert channel** before they can be enabled.
- The ticket forum requires a **Community server** so Discord allows forum channels. See [Getting Started](/getting-started).

**Users can't open tickets even though the system is on**

- You need at least one enabled [ticket category](/configuration/ticket-categories).

**A server is missing from the dashboard**

- You need the **Manage Server** permission there, and the bot must be in the server.

## Checklist

Before reporting a bug, quickly verify:

- [ ] The bot is in the server and online
- [ ] The relevant system is enabled and not paused
- [ ] The bot has the permissions it needs (and correct role order)
- [ ] The user's DMs are open and they authorized the bot
- [ ] The user isn't blacklisted
