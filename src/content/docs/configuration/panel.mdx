A **panel** is a message you place in a server channel with a button that members click to open a ticket. It's the friendliest way for users to reach support - they don't need to know any commands or that they can DM the bot.

## Creating a panel

Create and edit your panel in the <DashLink route="?next=/panel">Panel</DashLink> page of the dashboard. You configure:

- **Panel Data** - the message content members see (title and text), built with the panel editor.
- **Panel Actions** - the button(s) on the message. A button can open a ticket (`ticket:create`), or be a link.
- **Allowed Mentions** - which mentions in the panel message are allowed to ping.

<Aside type="note">
  Panel management moved to the dashboard - the old `/config panel` command no
  longer exists. Edit, save and send your panel entirely from the Panel page.
</Aside>

## Posting the panel

Choose the **channel** where the panel should appear and press send. If you move or lose the message, you can re-post it to a channel at any time - the panel stays linked to your configuration, so you don't have to rebuild it.

## Tips

- Put the panel in a channel every member can see, such as `#support` or `#help`.
- Keep the message short and tell users what to expect after clicking (that the bot will DM them).
- A panel is optional. Users can always open a ticket by DMing the bot directly.
