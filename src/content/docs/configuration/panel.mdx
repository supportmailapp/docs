A **panel** is a message you place in a server channel with a button that members click to open a ticket. It's the friendliest way for users to reach support - they don't need to know any commands or that they can DM the bot.

## Creating a panel

Create or edit your panel with the `/config panel` command in your server. You configure:

- **Panel Data** - the message content members see (title and text).
- **Panel Actions** - the button(s) that open a ticket.
- **Allowed Mentions** - which mentions in the panel message are allowed to ping.

<Aside type="note">
  Panel creation currently lives in the `/config panel` command. The dashboard
  shows your panel and lets you send it to a channel.
</Aside>

## Posting the panel

Choose the **channel** where the panel should appear and send it. If you move or lose the message, you can re-post it to a channel at any time - the panel stays linked to your configuration.

## Tips

- Put the panel in a channel every member can see, such as `#support` or `#help`.
- Keep the message short and tell users what to expect after clicking (that the bot will DM them).
- A panel is optional. Users can always open a ticket by DMing the bot directly.
