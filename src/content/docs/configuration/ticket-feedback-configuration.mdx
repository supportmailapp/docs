Feedback lets you ask users to rate their support experience when their ticket is closed. Configure it under <DashLink route="?next=/feedback">Feedback</DashLink>.

## Basic settings

- **Enable Feedback** — when on, the user receives a rating prompt in their DMs as the ticket closes.
- **Feedback Channel** — the channel where submitted feedback is posted so your team can review it.
- **Thank You Message** — shown to the user right after they submit (for example, _"Thank you for your feedback!"_).

## Custom questions

Beyond the star rating, you can add your own **custom questions** to learn more from users. Add, remove and reorder them on the feedback page — the order you set is the order users see.

## How it looks to the user

When a ticket closes with feedback enabled, the user gets a short note and rating controls in their DM. This also doubles as a closure notification — see [Handling Tickets](/managing-support/handling-tickets#closing-tickets). Submitted ratings and answers are posted to your feedback channel.

<Aside type="note">
  Keep it light — one rating plus at most a question or two gets far more responses than a long
  survey.
</Aside>
