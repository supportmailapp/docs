<Aside type="caution" title="Beta, limited access">
  Whitelabel is in beta and only available to a few servers for now. To request
  access, open a ticket in the [support server](https://help.supportmail.dev/).
  Whitelabel will become part of a future paid plan.
</Aside>

With whitelabel, SupportMail runs in your server as **your own bot**: your bot's name, avatar, banner and presence, with all of SupportMail's features behind it. Your bot replaces the main SupportMail bot in your server.

Everything is managed on the <DashLink route="?next=/whitelabel">Whitelabel</DashLink> page of the dashboard.

## Who can do what

- **Server owner only:** saving or replacing the bot token, activating your bot, and the switch-back options that close open tickets.
- **Anyone with dashboard access to the server:** starting, stopping and restarting the bot, changing its branding, and switching back when no tickets are open.

## Set up your bot

<Steps>

1. Create an application in the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications).

2. On the application's **Bot** page, turn on the **Message Content** intent.

3. Reset the bot token and copy it.

4. On the Whitelabel page, paste the token and click **Save token**. The token is sent to our server once, stored encrypted and never shown again.

5. Add your bot to your server with the **Add your bot to the server** button that appears once the token is saved.

6. Click **Activate your bot**. From then on your bot serves your server instead of the main SupportMail bot. Activation can take up to two minutes.

</Steps>

<Aside type="danger" title="Do not kick the main SupportMail bot">
  Keep the main SupportMail bot in your server after you activate your own bot.
  It does not need any permissions there, so you can take them away, but it
  must stay a member of the server.
</Aside>

When you activate, you can choose **Resend the panel automatically**. Your bot then posts the ticket panel again and deletes the old message. Without it, the old panel's buttons stop working until you send the panel again from the <DashLink route="?next=/panel">Panel</DashLink> page.

To replace the token later, stop the bot first, then save the new token.

## Start, stop and restart

The status card on the Whitelabel page shows whether your bot is running, its application ID and the number of open tickets.

- **Stop** takes your bot offline. While it is stopped, dashboard features that need the bot do not work, and the main SupportMail bot does not step in.
- **Start** brings it back.
- **Restart** stops and starts it in one go.

If the bot runs into a problem, for example its token stopped working or it was removed from your server, the status card says so.

## Branding

The **Branding** card sets how your bot looks on Discord:

- **Name, avatar, banner and About me.** Images can be PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 8 MiB.
- **Presence:** status (Online, Idle, Do not disturb, Invisible) and an activity (custom status, Playing, Listening to, Watching or Competing in).

Branding changes your bot's **global** Discord profile, the same one it shows everywhere. It works while the bot is stopped, and it is not touched when you switch back to the main bot.

## Switch back to the main bot

Click **Switch back to the main bot** on the Whitelabel page. Which options you get depends on whether tickets are open:

- **Switch now:** no tickets are open, so the main bot takes over right away.
- **Close tickets, then switch** (server owner only): your running bot closes the open tickets and tells their users, then the main bot takes over. This can take up to two minutes.
- **Force the switch** (server owner only): open tickets are closed in the database only. Users get no message and ticket threads stay as they are.

As with activation, you can let the main bot resend the ticket panel automatically.

## Direct messages

Your bot serves exactly one server: yours. That changes how users talk to it in DMs:

- A DM to your bot always goes to your server, so users never pick a server.
- Users do not need to [log in](/getting-support/contacting-support) first. Your bot checks their membership itself, so <SlashCommand name="login" /> and <SlashCommand name="logout" /> do not exist on whitelabel bots.
- The main SupportMail bot no longer offers your server in its DMs.
