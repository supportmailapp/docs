Reports let your members flag messages and other users for your mod team to review. You can turn reports on, decide where mods get notified, and control what (if anything) gets sent to users by DM. Set it all up under <DashLink route="?next=/report-settings">Report Settings</DashLink>. For how your mods actually handle incoming reports day-to-day, see [Managing Reports](/managing-support/managing-reports).

## Turning reports on

Before you can enable reports, pick an **alert channel** - that's where every new report shows up for your mods. Once that's set, flip the system on. You can also **pause** it at any time without losing your settings.

- **Automatic Resolution** - decide if reports close themselves right away, or stay open until a mod reviews and resolves them.

<Aside type="caution">
  Set an alert channel and sensible limits before you turn reports on, so your
  mods actually see new reports and don't get flooded.
</Aside>

## Where reports can be created

By default members can report from anywhere. If you want to restrict that, choose channels and pick one of:

- **Included** - reports can only be made in the channels you pick.
- **Excluded** - reports can be made everywhere except the channels you pick.

## Limits

Stop your report channel from getting spammed:

- **Max open reports per user** - how many reports one member can have open at the same time.
- **Max open reports in the server** - a total cap for the whole server.

## Notifications

- **Pings** - roles or people to ping in the alert channel whenever a new report comes in.

### DMs to users

SupportMail can also message users directly about reports. There are three separate DMs, each with its own on/off switch, so turn on only what you want:

- **DM on report creation** - lets the person who made the report know it went through. Sent right away.
- **DM on report resolution** - tells the reporter what happened once a mod resolves their report. You choose which outcomes send this DM (e.g. only for timeouts/kicks/bans, or also when a report is ignored or dismissed).
- **DM to the reported user** - lets someone know when a mod has taken action against them. This DM never says who reported them or which mod handled it. If the action is a kick or ban, it's sent right before it happens, so the person still gets it even if the kick/ban itself doesn't go through.

For the resolution and reported-user DMs, use **Sent for** to pick which outcomes trigger the message. For all three, use **Included info** to pick what details are in it - things like the reason given, a link to the reported message, or how long a timeout lasts. Options differ slightly per DM: for example the reported user's DM never reveals the reporter's identity or the moderator's name, no matter what you pick.
