Reports let members flag messages and users for your moderators. Configure the system under <DashLink route="?next=/report-settings">Report Settings</DashLink>. To learn how mods act on reports, see [Managing Reports](/managing-support/managing-reports).

## System Control

Turn the report system on/off or **pause** it. You must set an **alert channel** before you can enable it.

- **Automatic Resolution** — choose whether reports resolve instantly or stay open for a mod to review and resolve.

## Alert Channel

The channel where new reports are posted for your moderators. This is required for the system to run.

## Special Channels

Limit where reports can be created:

- **Included** — reports can _only_ be created in the selected channels.
- **Excluded** — reports _cannot_ be created in the selected channels.

## Limits

Protect your team from spam by capping open reports:

- **Max open reports per user** — how many a single user can have open at once.
- **Max open reports in the server** — a server-wide ceiling.

## Notifications

- **Pings** — roles and users to notify when a new report comes in.
- **User Notifications** — control what feedback the reporting user receives after submitting.

<Aside type="caution">
  Set the alert channel and reasonable limits before enabling reports, so moderators see submissions
  immediately and can't be flooded.
</Aside>
