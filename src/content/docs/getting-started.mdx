SupportMail is a modmail bot for Discord. Users contact your staff through DMs with the bot, and staff answer in a private forum channel that members can't see.
In about 5 minutes you will have a ticket forum, a panel members can click to ask for help, and a working test ticket.

<LinkButton href="https://supportmail.dev/invite" icon="external">
  Invite Bot
</LinkButton>

## Before you start

- You need the **Manage Server** permission on the server you want to add SupportMail to.
- Your server needs **Community** enabled, because tickets live in a forum channel and Discord only offers forums to Community servers. See [Enabling Your Community Server](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360047132851-Enabling-Your-Community-Server).
- Have at least one role for the staff members who will handle tickets.

## Setup

<Steps>

1. **Invite SupportMail to your server**

   Use the **Invite Bot** button above and pick your server.

2. **Run the setup wizard**

   Open your server in the <DashLink>dashboard</DashLink>. New servers start the setup wizard automatically. It walks you through your language, tickets (including the ticket forum and a panel) and reports.

   Prefer Discord? Run `/setup tickets wizard` and `/setup reports wizard` instead. They cover the same settings. For a ticket forum with default settings right away, use `/setup tickets quick`.

   <Aside type="note">
     Nothing is changed until you confirm on the final review screen. See the
     [Setup Wizard](/configuration/setup-wizard) page for every step.
   </Aside>

3. **Give your staff access**

   Once the forum exists, add your staff roles to it in Discord so they can see and answer tickets.

   <Aside type="caution">
     The bot's own permissions in the ticket forum are managed by the bot.
     **Don't change them manually. Please.**
   </Aside>

4. **Send a panel (optional)**

   Users who aren't Discord-savvy may not know they can DM the bot. A panel is a message with a button that starts a ticket. The setup wizard can send a ready-made one for you.

   To customize it, build and send your own from the <DashLink route="?next=/panel">Panel page</DashLink>. See [Panel](/configuration/panel) for the full walkthrough.

5. **Open a test ticket**

   Send a DM to the bot, or click the button on your panel. Check that the ticket appears in the forum and that a staff reply reaches you in DMs.

</Steps>

You're done. SupportMail is live on your server.

## Something not working?

| Problem                                 | Fix                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The ticket steps are unavailable        | Enable **Community** on your server. See [Before you start](#before-you-start).                                                                          |
| The wizard won't let you finish         | The bot is missing a permission it needs, for example to create the forum or to send in your panel channel. The review screen lists what is missing.     |
| The bot doesn't answer a DM             | The system may be paused or disabled, or the user is [blacklisted](/managing-support/blacklisting). Users also need open DMs and must authorize the bot. |
| Users can't open tickets                | Make sure the ticket system is enabled and not paused, and that at least one [ticket category](/configuration/ticket-categories) is enabled.             |
| Your server is missing in the dashboard | The bot must be in the server and you need **Manage Server** there.                                                                                      |

More fixes are on the [Common Issues](/troubleshooting/common-issues) page.
